Version: 0.8.0
Date: 2025-09-24
  Minor Features:
    - Added rails and gates to the castable items. Added railway as prerequisit techs to avoid cheesing rails.
    - Added technology for casting walls and gates.
    - Added casting of stone and steel furncances. In case you need hundreds of stone furncances in the late game.
  Balancing:
    - In complex mode, sand-to-lava now does not require a calcite anymore.
    - In complex mode, lava-to-stone now requires calcite.
    - Raised crafting time for casting bricks from 12 to 24 seconds to match the other casting recipes.
  Other:
    - Updated dependency to OCs_base_assets 0.3.0
    - Rearranging mod files and use more of my base assets mod
    - Renaming internal recipe names by removing the "-recipe" suffix.
  Compatibility:
    - ["AAI Industry"] casting-wall-tech now requires steel wall tech, but unlocks all wall recipes.
    - Added compatibility with "Concrete Productivity" mod.
    - Added small combatibility with "Metal and Stars" mod. Only the casting rail recipe is affected.
    - Started adding compatibility with "Kastorio 2 Spaced out" mod. Sand, quatz and glass are supported now.
